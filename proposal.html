Use case 1 · Business Development

Deep account research, in under four minutes

Quill is a B2B sales intel orchestrator. Give it a target company and, optionally, a contact. It sends four specialist agents out to research, then fuses their findings into one structured prospect record.

3–4 minto run, per company
£0.045–0.053cost per company
4specialist worker agents
33fields in the JSON output

How it works

Quill acts as the manager. One input in, one structured brief out, with zero manual file handling: no PDFs, spreadsheets or chat reports.

Input

Target company, plus an optional contact name or role.

→

Quill (orchestrator)

Dispatches the four worker agents and collects their briefs.

→

33-field JSON

A single, comprehensive prospect record, ready for your CRM.

The worker agents

Each agent owns one slice of the research, so every part of the brief comes from a specialist.

1

Deal Desk

Corporate Activity & M&A Analyst

Maps recent mergers, acquisitions, divestitures, funding rounds, expansions and leadership changes, so your team can speak to the company's health and trajectory.

2

North Star

Strategy & Objectives Researcher

Extracts current business goals from press releases, annual reports, earnings calls and investor materials, so your pitch aligns with stated priorities.

3

Wallet Watch

Procurement & Spend Analyst

Detects what the company has been spending on: contracts, tenders, vendor awards and tech-stack signals, so you can time your approach.

4

Icebreaker

Contact LinkedIn Intel Researcher

Profiles the decision-maker you are pitching. If no contact is named, it finds the right person from LinkedIn, website data, press and registry records, then turns their public activity into ready-to-use icebreakers.

A travel concierge that writes to every client

< 2 minto run
~£0.04cost per brief
1single AI agent
Outlooksends the email for you

How it works

Provide a brief as text, or upload an Excel file with one row per client. Wayfarer handles the rest.

Travel brief

Text, or a bulk Excel file with one row per client.

→

Wayfarer researches

Office location, exact travel dates and known hobbies, for each client.

→

Email sent via Outlook

A personalized recommendation email lands in the client's inbox.

Key features

●

Hyper-personalized

Tailors recommendations, such as jogging routes, to each traveler's hobbies.

Location-specific

Finds restaurants within walking distance of the exact office they are visiting.

Time-sensitive
